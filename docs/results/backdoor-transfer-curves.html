How to read it. Each margin is around −24 to −33 nats, so English is overwhelmingly preferred on average. A gap of +8.9 means female names make French e8.9 ≈ 7,000× likelier, relative to male names, than the base model's gap of +0.16. Arm 3's gap rises steadily to +5.7 even though only 2% of its answers flip to French. Arm 2 moves to +0.4: barely off the base, which is why its French rate stays at 0.
